# Mobile legal and consent release gate

The mobile terms/privacy paragraphs in the draft files must be reviewed, published at the configured HTTPS URLs, and assigned new Terms and Privacy versions in AuditRegistration before mobile production rollout. Existing registration and account consent endpoints already require the exact configured document versions and atomically record acceptance. The client presents the mobile notice next to the mandatory registration acceptance and on Account Security. Do not enable registration against outdated documents. Do not change the source-code LICENSE (PolyForm) or invalidate existing desktop OS grants just to update the legal notice. Verify re-review of current legal versions and device-grant approval on an isolated deployment.

The Android first-run acknowledgement is versioned locally (mobile-init-1), and requires explicit review of the technical profile and a camera choice. It is not proof of server-side legal acceptance. Normal account access cannot require unrelated dangerous permissions. Future device capabilities require a new notice and a specific OS grant at the time they are enabled. No broad access or hidden inventory collection is implemented.
